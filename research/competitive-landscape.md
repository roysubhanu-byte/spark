# Spark Competitive Landscape — April 2026

## The Mega Gap Nobody Fills

After researching 20+ tools, here's what NOBODY does:

| Gap | Details |
|-----|---------|
| No "swipe to discover" UX | Every tool is search-based or list-based. Nobody uses a Tinder mechanic for business ideas. |
| No single tool goes idea → first sale | Every tool covers ONE slice. Nobody chains discovery → validation → sourcing → first order. |
| Beginners are abandoned | Most tools assume you already have a store, know your market, picked your model. |
| No cross-model discovery | Etsy tools for Etsy, Amazon tools for Amazon. Nobody helps discover the right MODEL first. |
| Idea discovery is SaaS-biased | Ideabrowser, BigIdeasDB focus on software. Physical products ignored. |
| No personalized matching | Nobody says "given your $500 budget + design skills + 10hrs/week, here are your 5 best options." |

## Key Takeaway

Nobody owns the "I want to start a business but don't know what" moment.

Every competitor starts at step 3+ (you already have a product/store/idea). Spark starts at step 0.

The swipe mechanic is genuinely novel in this space. No competitor uses it.
